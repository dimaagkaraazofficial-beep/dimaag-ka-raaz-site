Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who we are

This policy covers "Dimaag Ka Raaz Uploader" (the "App"), a personal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Dimaag Ka Raaz (@DimaagKaRaazOfficial). Contact: dimaagkaraaz.official@gmail.com.

What the App does

The App uses the YouTube Data API (scope youtube.upload) to upload and schedule videos on the channel owner's own YouTube channel.

Data we access and store

  • A Google OAuth token for the channel owner's own account, stored only on the owner's own computer or in the owner's private, encrypted automation secrets. It is used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • The App does not collect, store, sell, share or transfer personal data of any other user, viewer or subscriber.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking is used by the App.

Use of Google user data

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the upload feature described above and is not used for advertising or sold to anyone.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. To delete stored tokens, delete the token file on the owner's computer or remove the secret from the automation settings.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: dimaagkaraaz.official@gmail.com.
